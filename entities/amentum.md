# Amentum

**Type:** Organization (`NYSE: AMTM`) | **Industry:** Defense / Government Contracting / Staffing

## Overview

Amentum provides staffing, engineering, and program management services to government agencies including NASA and defense organizations. It absorbed Jacobs Critical Mission Solutions (CMS) in late 2024, creating a ~53,000-employee government services giant with a **$48.2B total backlog** as of Q3 FY2026 ^[raw/articles/gemini-career-outlook-2026-10-16.md].

## Rick's Role

Amentum is Rick's **staffing contractor**, not his direct customer. Rick is currently on assignment at [[entities/nasa]] (MSFC) via Amentum contract.

- **Contract:** ESSCA (NASA engineering/science support)
- **Salary:** $170,000 (started at $130,000 + $5,000 signing bonus in March 2019; annual raise cycle expected ~November 2026) ^[raw/articles/gemini-career-outlook-2026-10-16.md]
- **Bonus history:** Only $200–$300/yr since joining; earned less in Year 2 than Year 1 due to <3% raise and no signing bonus, and earned less every year since 2023 than in 2023 (when he worked heavy overtime before caring for dying parents in 2024–2025) ^[raw/articles/gemini-career-outlook-2026-10-16.md]
- **Health insurance:** Family coverage moved onto Amentum's plan effective **August 1, 2026** (after wife's coverage ended July 31, 2026) ^[raw/articles/gemini-career-outlook-2026-10-16.md]
- **Leave:** 172 hours/year
- **401(k):** ~50% match of 6% contribution (~$5,100/yr)
- **Growth ceiling:** Rick sees limited advancement opportunity at Amentum

## ESSCA Contract Status

- **Proposals due:** 11 March 2026 — **ESSCA II performance start delayed to ~May 2027, with ESSCA I extended to bridge the gap** (confirmed 2026-10-11)
- Contract is **up for re-bid** — could change contractors, though Amentum is favored to retain
- Re-bid may adjust rates closer to inflation (ERI benchmark for ES-8 is $84.46/hr vs. Rick's $81.42/hr; see [[queries/essca-ii-rfp-analysis]])
- Risk: new contractor could restructure, change terms, or displace existing staff
- Rick's remote work arrangement (~50%) depends on contractor/team flexibility

## NASA Insourcing Pressure & Financial Outlook (Oct 2026)

- **Q3 FY2026 Earnings Warning:** Amentum explicitly guided for a **3% revenue headwind in FY2027** (~$400M–$420M top-line drag) directly tied to NASA's **workforce insourcing directive**, which brings lower-margin cost-plus staffing positions in-house as civil servants ^[raw/articles/gemini-career-outlook-2026-10-16.md]
- **Stock & Backlog:** `AMTM` shares fell ~30%–33% YTD in 2026 into the low-to-mid $20s on insourcing and contract transition concerns, though offset by a $48.2B backlog and major space/infrastructure wins ($1.8B NASA COSMOS JV at JSC, $973.8M Langley CMOE II, and $317M MSFC FOMSS II JV) ^[raw/articles/gemini-career-outlook-2026-10-16.md]
- Rick strongly opposes converting to NASA civil service (see [[queries/nasa-civil-service-gs-pay]])

## Post-Jacobs Merger HR & Payroll Friction (Aug–Oct 2026)

- Following the Jacobs CMS merger, Amentum's migrated HR/benefits and payroll systems have exhibited significant bureaucratic friction ^[raw/articles/gemini-career-outlook-2026-10-16.md]
- **August 1, 2026 Benefits Overcharge (~$2,000):** When wife's insurance ended July 31, 2026, Rick enrolled the family in Amentum benefits effective August 1, 2026. Because he initiated the paperwork ahead of time on **June 23, 2026**, Amentum's payroll system erroneously backdated coverage to June 23 – August 1 and shorted his paycheck by **nearly $2,000**, promising a refund in "one or two cycles" without a firm date (as of 2026-10-16, hoping it lands on the **Thursday, October 22, 2026** paycheck) ^[raw/articles/gemini-career-outlook-2026-10-16.md]
