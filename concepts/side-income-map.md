## Ruled out, with the reasons (so they do not come back)

| Idea | Why not |
|---|---|
| **Investing** to cover a cash-flow deficit | The debt costs ~6.8%; safe yields ~4%. Paying the debt *is* the better risk-adjusted position |
| **Buying and reselling** online | Needs working capital that costs 6.8% here, and ranks below capital-free work |
| **Power Apps / low-code** consulting | Rick does not want to do it, government tenants constrain it, and it is capability rather than an offer |
| **AI work for government or defence networks** | IA review, no external model calls, no data egress - the slowest possible first dollar |
| **Bidding on freelance marketplaces** | Medians run ~$27-30/hr, a quarter of the target rate. Platforms are discovery, not the rate |
| **Toptal** | One failed screen, no retry |

## The rule that keeps this honest

Every thread is judged on **time to first dollar**, and the asset-building bets are **capped** so they
cannot consume the hours that pay 3-10x faster. When a thread's status changes, update the page it lives
on — never this map.
